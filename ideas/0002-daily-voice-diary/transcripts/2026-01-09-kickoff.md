## Ideas & Themes

- **Capture First, Curate Later**: The impulse to share a thought shouldn't require interrupting someone. Stream everything into a persistent thread; summarization and routing happen after the fact, not before. This inverts the typical messaging workflow where you decide audience and format upfront.

- **Talking to Computers as Primary Interface**: A larger intuition that we're moving toward continuous conversation with computers. "Messaging" as a distinct act may give way to ambient capture with intelligent routing and compilation. This diary is a narrow, practical test of that idea.

- **Condensation Categories**: Partner summaries need filtering by context; what's relevant differs by role and moment. Five categories emerged: Personal (how the day felt), Business/Admin (partner/owner level), Technical (architecture, features, security), Data (data model considerations), and Money (future; contracts and financials).

- **Slack's Structure vs Input Method**: Slack gets the structure right (threads, humans, groups) but the input method is broken. The friction of deciding who to message and when creates an obstacle to capturing thoughts as they arise.

## Transcript Summary

### The Problem with Messaging

The impulse to share a thought with a dev partner shouldn't require making a decision about interrupting them. Slack's organizational structure works fine; it's the input method that creates friction. You have to decide: is this worth sending now? Should I batch it? The thought gets lost or diluted.

### Capture Everything Philosophy

"Capture the stream, curate later." The raw stream of thoughts throughout the day becomes the source material. Voice first; don't filter at the point of capture. Transcripts preserve the full context; summaries can be generated as needed for different purposes.

### The Larger Intuition

This is part of a bigger idea about how interaction with computers is changing. The concept of "messaging" someone specific might evolve into talking continuously and having the system route and compile appropriately. This daily voice diary is a narrow test: one primary audience (dev partner), end of day compilation, multiple condensation modes.

### Condensation for Partner Summaries

When generating summaries for a dev partner, different lenses matter at different times:

1. **Personal**: How the day felt, how work went, human connection notes
2. **Business/Admin**: Partner/owner level stuff, administrative
3. **Technical**: Architecture, features rolled out, security concerns, general thoughts
4. **Data**: Data model, structure, design considerations
5. **Money** (future): Contracts, sales, financials when relevant

### Intended Workflow

The workflow is: talk into a thread throughout the day (thoughts, progress, questions, ideas), generate a transcript at end of day to capture the raw stream, generate summaries as views when needed (daily, multi-day, weekly), and condense for partner using the categories above; could be full thread, bullet points, or filtered by topic.
