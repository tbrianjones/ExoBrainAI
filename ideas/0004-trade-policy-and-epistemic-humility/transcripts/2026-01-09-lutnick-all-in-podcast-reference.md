# Howard Lutnick on All-In Podcast: Trade Policy Reference

**Date**: January 9, 2026
**Source**: All-In Podcast

**Interviewee**: Howard Lutnick, U.S. Commerce Secretary

## Verified Quotes on Steel and Subsidies

### US Steel Costs
> "How much does it cost in America? $700 a metric ton."

### Subsidized Foreign Steel
> "Japan subsidizes, Korea subsidizes it. So maybe they can do it for $400 a metric ton."

### Chinese Steel with Free Power
> "If the Chinese give power, G-I-V-E their power to the steel producer, free... you'd be making your steel for $250 a metric ton."

### Steel Industry Decline
> "We start with 40 steel blast furnaces. Now we have 10. And if we kept going, we were going to have zero, zero."

### National Security Framing
> "If you need steel, you want to build your own missiles, you have to call another country."

### On the Playing Field
> "It's amazing. The subsidies, the assistance. It's not a level playing field."

## Context

Lutnick's argument structure:
1. Foreign governments subsidize production (steel, semiconductors)
2. This allows them to undercut US producers
3. US industries decline (40 blast furnaces → 10)
4. This creates strategic dependency
5. Tariffs are meant to level the playing field and incentivize reshoring
