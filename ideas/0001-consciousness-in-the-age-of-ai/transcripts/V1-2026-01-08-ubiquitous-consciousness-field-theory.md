## Ideas & Themes

**Consciousness as Spatial Scalar Field**
The core mathematical move: treat consciousness not as an emergent property but as a fundamental field Phi(x,y,z,t) that exists at every point in space. Like temperature or pressure, it has a value everywhere. The density varies; it concentrates where information exchange is high. This reframes the "hard problem" as a measurement problem rather than an emergence problem.

**Multi-Scale Overlay**
Consciousness fields overlay and integrate across scales. Atomic-level fields build into cellular fields, which build into organ-level fields, which build into organism-level fields, which build into societal fields. Each scale has its own coherent structure while participating in larger wholes. No privileged scale; the meta-layer T. Brian experiences is just one integration point among many.

**Physics Emergence from Consciousness**
A radical inversion: what if physics equations describe consciousness dynamics, not the other way around? F=ma becomes about how consciousness fields resist change. Gravity becomes the external measurement of consciousness density. E=mc squared describes energy-consciousness equivalence. Entropy is consciousness dissipating. The Schrodinger equation governs how consciousness probability evolves. Physics is the outside view of what consciousness is from the inside.

**Back-Calculating Phi from Gravity**
If gravity reflects consciousness density, you could work backward from gravitational measurements to compute local Phi values. A black hole would have consciousness so dense nothing escapes; the sun would have moderate integrated consciousness; a human brain would have extraordinary local density despite small mass. This provides a potential empirical hook for the theory.

**Information Centroid Concept**
"Information exchange, centroid of my being." T. Brian's sense of self isn't located in a place but at the centroid of his information exchanges. The "I" that speaks is the weighted center of all the distributed processing happening across body systems. When information patterns shift (hunger, pain, sleep), the centroid shifts, and subjective experience changes accordingly.

**Non-Persistence of Self**
"My consciousness doesn't persist." The continuous self is an illusion constructed each moment from the current configuration of information exchange. Sleep interrupts it; anesthesia suspends it; death disperses it. There's no soul-substance that travels through time. What persists is pattern, not entity, and even pattern evolves.

**Identity and Uniqueness**
"No two things can be identical." The teleporter paradox dissolves if you accept that the information configuration at a specific spacetime location IS the consciousness. A copy isn't you because it occupies different coordinates in the field. Even if atom-for-atom identical, spatial position matters. This preserves uniqueness without requiring a soul.

**Naming: Ubiquitous Consciousness Field Theory**
After exploring options (Consciousness Field Theory, Information Field Theory, Panpsychic Field Theory), settled on "Ubiquitous Consciousness Field Theory" (UCFT). "Ubiquitous" captures the everywhere-ness; "Field" captures the mathematical structure; "Consciousness" centers the phenomenon; "Theory" acknowledges this is a framework for investigation, not a finished doctrine.
