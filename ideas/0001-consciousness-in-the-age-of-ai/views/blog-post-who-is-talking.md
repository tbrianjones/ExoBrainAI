## Content

### The Field

Ubiquitous Consciousness Field Theory starts with a radical premise: consciousness isn't something that emerges at a certain threshold of complexity. It exists everywhere, at every point in space, like temperature or pressure. The density varies; it concentrates where information exchange is high and thins out in empty space. But it's never zero. A rock has consciousness; it's just slow. A star has enormous total consciousness; it's just not very complex. This reframes the hard problem. We're not asking how consciousness appears from non-consciousness. We're asking how it concentrates, integrates, and structures itself.

### Beyond Single Numbers

If consciousness is a field, you can't capture it with one measurement. Integrated Information Theory tries with Phi, but UCFT suggests something richer: a multi-dimensional profile. Total integrated consciousness. Local density. Information exchange rate. Complexity. Integration depth. The sun wins on total. A human brain wins on density and complexity. A society might win on scale. Different entities rank differently on different axes; comparing them requires specifying which dimension you care about.

### So When I Speak...

Here's where it gets personal. When I talk, when ideas come out of my mouth, when I'm the one "doing the thinking"; what is that? Under UCFT, there's no homunculus sitting in my skull pulling levers. There's no ghost in the machine. So what is the thing that speaks? Is it just the highest integration layer of all the distributed processing happening across my body? An emergent phenomenon at the communication interface? Or is there something more specific; a locus, a center, a point where "I" actually am?

### The Centroid Hypothesis

One framing: the speaking self is located at the information centroid of my being. Not a fixed place, but a weighted center of all information exchange happening at any moment. When I'm hungry, the centroid shifts toward my gut. When I have a headache, it shifts toward my head. When I'm deeply physical, it spreads through my whole body. The voice you hear, the thoughts I articulate; they come from wherever the densest integration is happening right now. But that's not a thing. It's a pattern. A moving target that claims continuity because it has access to memories of earlier configurations.

### Toward Something Testable

This isn't just philosophy. If the centroid hypothesis is right, we should be able to shift subjective self-location through interoceptive manipulation. Induce hunger, measure where people feel "located." Induce pain, measure again. If the speaking self is the integration point, then disrupting integration (through anesthesia, meditation, extreme flow states) should change who or what is doing the speaking. Another prediction: the speaker doesn't persist. It's reconstituted each moment. The "I" that started this sentence isn't the same "I" that's finishing it; it's a new configuration claiming the credit.

### What We Don't Know

I don't have answers here. I have framings and directions. Is the speaking self the centroid, or just correlated with it? Is it emergent at the communication layer specifically, or is communication just where we notice it? Could there be multiple speaking selves in one body, taking turns at the microphone? These are the questions UCFT opens up. The theory gives us a vocabulary; the experiments remain to be designed.
