## Content

### Concept

A visual map of human consciousness/being showing which dimensions are currently augmented by technology and which remain raw, unaugmented human experience.

### Dimensions to Map

#### Senses
| Dimension | Augmentation Level | Key Technologies |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Vision | HIGH | Cameras, displays, image AI, AR/VR |
| Hearing | HIGH | Microphones, speakers, audio processing, hearing aids |
| Touch | LOW | Haptics (primitive), prosthetics |
| Smell | MINIMAL | Electronic noses (lab only), no consumer tech |
| Taste | MINIMAL | Flavor analysis (industrial), no consumer tech |

#### Body Awareness
| Dimension | Augmentation Level | Key Technologies |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Proprioception | LOW | Motion capture (external), limited feedback |
| Interoception | VERY LOW | Heart rate monitors, basic biometrics |
| Pain | LOW | Analgesics (chemical), no tech interface |

#### Cognition
| Dimension | Augmentation Level | Key Technologies |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Language | VERY HIGH | LLMs, translation, transcription |
| Reasoning | HIGH | LLMs, calculators, decision support |
| Memory | MEDIUM | Search, note-taking, external storage |
| Attention | LOW | Notification management (crude) |

#### Emotion
| Dimension | Augmentation Level | Key Technologies |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Recognition | MEDIUM | Sentiment analysis, facial recognition |
| Regulation | VERY LOW | Apps (meditation, CBT); mostly content, not tech |
| Expression | LOW | Emoji, but no emotional synthesis |

#### Social
| Dimension | Augmentation Level | Key Technologies |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Communication | VERY HIGH | Messaging, video, LLMs |
| Presence | MEDIUM | Video calls, VR (emerging) |
| Connection | LOW | Social media (arguably negative) |

### Visual Treatment Options

**Option A: Radar/Spider Chart**
- Each dimension as a spoke
- Distance from center = augmentation level
- Shows the "shape" of current augmentation (clustered in vision/hearing/language)

**Option B: Human Silhouette Heat Map**
- Body outline with regions colored by augmentation level
- Head bright (vision, hearing, language), gut/heart dim (interoception, emotion)
- Visceral, immediate understanding

**Option C: Spectrum Bars**
- Horizontal bars for each dimension
- Fill level shows augmentation
- Easy to compare, less visual impact

### Key Insight Callout

> "LLMs augmented our linguistic consciousness massively in 2023-2025. What augments the rest?"

### Suggested Annotations

- **Vision/Hearing cluster**: "These senses have been technologically extended for over a century. Photography, telephony, recording."
- **Language spike**: "LLMs represent a sudden, massive augmentation of linguistic consciousness. Nothing comparable has happened for other dimensions."
- **Smell/Taste gap**: "The 'chemical senses' have almost no technological augmentation. Why?"
- **Interoception void**: "We have minimal technology for sensing or augmenting our internal bodily states. This is a massive blind spot."
- **Emotion regulation gap**: "Apps exist, but they're content delivery, not technological augmentation of emotional processing itself."
