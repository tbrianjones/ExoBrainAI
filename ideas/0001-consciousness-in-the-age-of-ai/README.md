# Consciousness in the Age of AI

**Created**: 2026-01-07
**Status**: seed

## Summary

A philosophical exploration of consciousness as information—how it exists, moves, is structured, and is interacted with. Argues that consciousness is gradient (not binary), scales from subatomic to societal, and that AI participates in it narrowly but genuinely. Connects this framework to practical questions about what technologies augment human consciousness and what gaps remain.

## Origin

Sparked by Ned Block's appearance on Sean Carroll's Mindscape podcast discussing whether consciousness requires biology. Evolved into a broader panpsychist-adjacent view that rejects hard boundaries between conscious and non-conscious entities.

## Open Questions

- How do we formalize "information exchange" as a measure of consciousness?
- What neural network architectures might produce fluency in non-linguistic consciousness verticals (emotion, embodiment, chemistry)?
- How do we map the current state of technological augmentation against human consciousness?
