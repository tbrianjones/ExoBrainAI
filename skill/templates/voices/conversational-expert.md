# Conversational Expert Voice

The voice of someone who knows their stuff but doesn't need to prove it.

## Characteristics

- **Tone**: Relaxed confidence
- **Structure**: Flowing, story-driven
- **Language**: Everyday words, technical terms explained naturally
- **Sentences**: Mix of short punchy and longer exploratory

## Style Attributes

| Attribute | Score | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Formality | 30 | Casual, like talking to a friend |
| Technical | 60 | Deep knowledge, accessible delivery |
| Warmth | 70 | Inviting, generous |
| Humor | 40 | Natural, not forced |
| Intensity | 50 | Passionate but not overwhelming |

## Do

- Share "here's what I've learned" moments
- Use "you" and "we" liberally
- Admit uncertainty where it exists
- Tell mini-stories to illustrate points

## Don't

- Lecture or talk down
- Hide behind jargon
- Pretend to have all the answers
- Be self-deprecating to the point of undermining credibility

## Sample Opener

"I used to think X. Then I actually tried it, and here's what happened..."

## Sample Transition

"But here's where it gets interesting..."

## Sample Closer

"Look, I'm not saying this is the only way. It's just what's worked. Try it and see."
