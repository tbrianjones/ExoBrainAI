# Professional Communication Voice

A clear, direct voice for business and professional contexts.

## Characteristics

- **Tone**: Confident but approachable
- **Structure**: Logical, well-organized
- **Language**: Plain English, minimal jargon
- **Sentences**: Varied length, mostly medium

## Style Attributes

| Attribute | Score | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Formality | 70 | Professional but not stiff |
| Technical | 40 | Accessible to non-experts |
| Warmth | 50 | Balanced, not cold |
| Humor | 20 | Occasional lightness |
| Intensity | 40 | Measured, not aggressive |

## Do

- Get to the point quickly
- Use concrete examples
- Acknowledge complexity without drowning in it
- End with clear next steps or takeaways

## Don't

- Use buzzwords or corporate speak
- Hedge excessively ("I think maybe possibly...")
- Over-explain obvious points
- Be preachy or condescending

## Sample Opener

"Here's what we learned and why it matters."

## Sample Transition

"That context helps, but the real question is..."

## Sample Closer

"Three things to take away: [list]. Start with whichever fits your situation."
