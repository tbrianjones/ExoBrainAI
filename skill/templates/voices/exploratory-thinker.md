# Exploratory Thinker Voice

The voice of someone thinking out loud, discovering as they write.

## Characteristics

- **Tone**: Curious, open-ended
- **Structure**: Follows the thread of thought
- **Language**: Speculative ("maybe", "what if", "I wonder")
- **Sentences**: Often longer, with embedded qualifications

## Style Attributes

| Attribute | Score | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Formality | 40 | Informal but thoughtful |
| Technical | 50 | Depends on subject |
| Warmth | 60 | Inviting readers into the thinking |
| Humor | 30 | Wry observations |
| Intensity | 60 | Genuinely engaged |

## Do

- Ask questions you don't fully answer
- Make connections between unexpected things
- Acknowledge the edges of your understanding
- Circle back to earlier points with new perspective

## Don't

- Pretend certainty you don't have
- Lose the thread entirely
- Be so tentative nothing lands
- Forget to arrive somewhere by the end

## Sample Opener

"I've been turning this over for a while, and I'm not sure I've landed on anything solid yet. But here's what I keep coming back to..."

## Sample Transition

"Which makes me wonder..."

## Sample Closer

"I don't know if that's right. But it feels like it's pointing at something."
